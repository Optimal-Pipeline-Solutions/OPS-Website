About Optimal Pipeline Solutions

We've been building control systems since before most platforms existed.

Optimal Pipeline Solutions is a remote monitoring, SCADA engineering, and compliance services company built by people who have spent their careers inside the industries they serve.

What makes us different

Most control room service providers come from one background. They know pipelines, and that's what they do. We started somewhere else entirely.

Before OPS existed, our engineering team was designing and commissioning control systems in plastics extrusion plants, pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities, automotive press lines, chemical batch processes, food production, emissions control systems, power generation, water treatment, recycling operations, and 24/7 continuous-process environments producing consumer products at industrial scale. We built enterprise SCADA systems from the ground up, managed teams of engineers across multi-site deployments, and solved problems in environments where downtime was measured in thousands of dollars per minute.

Then we brought all of that into the pipeline industry.

That's the difference. When a customer brings us a challenge, we don't just know pipelines — we know processes. We've seen what works in a manufacturing plant that runs three shifts with zero tolerance for unplanned stops, and we apply those same disciplines to pipeline gathering systems, terminal operations, and remote SCADA infrastructure. The result is a level of operational thinking that a pipeline-only team simply doesn't carry.

Our Team

The people behind the systems.

Christopher Babb

Principal SCADA Engineer & Founder

40 years in automation and controls engineering. AA in Electrical Engineering. 25 years as a systems integrator designing custom control solutions across dozens of industries. Thirteen years as the plant controls and SCADA engineer for a 24/7 consumer products manufacturer, overseeing full Ignition implementations and teams of electricians across two facilities. Holds a Principal Controls Engineer title from one of the largest system integration firms in the country.

Chris brings deep control room SCADA expertise and the custom software platform that powers OPS operations — alarm rationalization, automated compliance reporting, and intelligent data analysis.

John Brown

Manager of OT and Integrations

17 years in control system design, project management, and commissioning. BS in Mechanical Engineering and PMP certification. Focused on midstream pipeline control systems — pumping stations, custody transfer metering, storage facilities, and terminals — with complexity ranging from manual control to fully automated, unmanned facilities.

John has managed multi-terminal control center implementations, led teams of up to seven engineers on simultaneous site deployments, and delivered projects for some of the largest names in midstream operations. He brings the project management discipline and field commissioning experience that keeps complex deployments on schedule.

Joshua Davis

Senior Control Systems Engineer

8 years of control systems experience in liquids and gas, with a BA in Computer Engineering. Specializes in PLC programming, high-performance HMI development, SQL database design, and communications infrastructure. Has rebuilt tank vapor combustion control systems, developed pump station upgrades with split-range VFD control, migrated legacy PLC platforms to modern ControlLogix architectures, and standardized multi-site HMI environments.

Josh brings hands-on field engineering and a methodical approach to system standardization that ensures every site operates on the same proven foundation.
