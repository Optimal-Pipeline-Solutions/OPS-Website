About Optimal Pipeline Solutions

Building control systems since the first SCADA platforms came into being.

Your operation doesn't stop, and neither do we. Optimal Pipeline Solutions provides 24/7 third-party control room operations, SCADA and automation engineering, and full regulatory compliance management for pipeline, gathering, terminal, and storage operators. Our people have sat where your controllers sit, designed and commissioned the pumping stations and metering systems your product flows through, built the SCADA systems your operators rely on, answered the phone at 2 AM, stood across from PHMSA inspectors, and worked the field long before they worked the control room. When you hand us your operation, you're handing it to people who understand every part of it — because they've done every part of it.

What makes us different

Most control room service providers come from one background. They know pipelines, and that's what they do. We started somewhere else entirely.

Our engineering team has been building control systems since the first generation of SCADA platforms — back when the tools didn't do what you needed and you had to build the missing pieces yourself. Over four decades, our team has designed and commissioned control systems across more than 20 industries — from plastics extrusion and pharmaceutical manufacturing to automotive assembly, chemical processing, food production, power generation, and 24/7 continuous-process facilities running at industrial scale. We've built enterprise SCADA systems from the ground up, managed teams of engineers across multi-site deployments, and solved problems in environments where downtime was measured in thousands of dollars per minute.

We have brought all of that experience into pipeline operations. Every control center we design, every compliance system we build, every site we bring online carries the weight of everything that came before it. That kind of depth doesn't come from a textbook — it comes from decades of delivering systems that had to work the first time, every time.

That's the difference. When a customer brings us a challenge, we don't just know pipelines — we know processes. The result is a level of operational thinking that a pipeline-only team simply doesn't carry.

Our Capabilities

What we deliver.

You need a partner who can staff your control room around the clock, respond the moment something goes wrong, keep your compliance documentation ahead of every audit cycle, and engineer the systems that tie it all together — without you having to manage five different vendors to get there. That's exactly what we built this company to do, and every capability listed below exists because an operator like you needs it and we can deliver.

24/7 Monitoring & Callout

Round-the-clock remote monitoring of pipeline, gathering, and terminal operations. When an alarm fires at 2 AM, our controllers are already watching. When a callout is needed, it happens immediately — not after a paging chain. Your operation is staffed and covered every hour of every day, with no gaps in coverage and zero missed shifts.

Control Room Operations

Our controllers don't just watch screens — they come from pipeline control rooms and oil field operations. They understand the equipment, the processes, and the regulatory environment from the operator's seat. That operational depth shapes every decision they make and every system we design around them.

CRM Compliance Management

The full PHMSA compliance stack — ISA-18.2 alarm rationalization, monthly alarm performance reviews, annual alarm reviews, safety-related alarm reviews, controller fatigue management, point-to-point verification, and revision-controlled document management. Our compliance team has managed these programs since the mid-2010s, and purpose-built tooling generates and tracks every deliverable automatically.

Compliance Consulting

Third-party compliance consulting for operators who need expertise without a long-term contract. CRM plan development and review, mock audits, gap analysis, documentation assessment, and direct audit representation. Our team has sat across from PHMSA inspectors as the company representative — we know exactly what they look for and how to be ready for it.

SCADA & Automation Engineering

Multi-monitor Ignition SCADA implementations for control centers. PLC programming, panel design, and field commissioning for pumping stations, custody transfer metering, storage terminals, and unmanned facilities. ControlLogix, Modicon, GE, Siemens, SCADAPack — interfaced to Omni flow computers, Raptor tank gauging, and VFDs from every major manufacturer. We engineer it, we integrate it, and we commission it.

Custom Operational Tooling

When the off-the-shelf tools don't exist, we build them. Our proprietary operations platform handles alarm rationalization, automated compliance reporting, controller scheduling, document control, and operational data analysis — including intelligent tooling that helps us analyze millions of records and surface what matters. All developed in-house, specifically for pipeline and industrial operations.
