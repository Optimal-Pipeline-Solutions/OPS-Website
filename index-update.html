49 CFR 195.446
&
192.631
195.446(b) — Written Procedures
Written Control Room Management Procedures
Each operator must have and follow written procedures that implement all requirements of the CRM rule, revision-controlled and available to controllers during their watch.
OPS develops, maintains, and tracks revisions to all CRMP documentation with full audit history.
195.446(c) — Alarm Management
Alarm Management & ISA-18.2 Rationalization
Operators must have a program to identify and manage each alarm, reduce unnecessary alarms, and provide controllers with actionable information per API RP 1167 and ISA-18.2.
OPS performs full ISA-18.2 alarm rationalization, monthly alarm performance reviews, and annual alarm reviews — documented automatically.
195.446(d) — Change Management
Management of Change for SCADA & Systems
Whenever a SCADA system is added, expanded, or replaced, operators must implement API RP 1165 for display design and conduct point-to-point verification between SCADA displays and field equipment.
OPS manages all SCADA changes through a documented MOC process, including point-to-point verification with full sign-off documentation.
195.446(e) — Fatigue Management
Controller Fatigue Management
Operators must develop a fatigue management plan and educate controllers on the effects of fatigue. Shift scheduling must be designed to reduce fatigue-related errors in human performance.
OPS tracks controller hours, enforces fatigue management protocols on every shift, and documents compliance in the shift log — automatically.
195.446(f) — Training
Controller Training & Qualification
Each operator must establish and implement a controller training program. Controllers must be qualified per API RP 1168 and retrained whenever systems or procedures change.
All OPS controllers are API RP 1168 qualified. Training records are updated whenever a SCADA change or procedure revision occurs.
195.446(g) — Shift Handover
Shift Handover & Documentation
Operators must implement procedures for when a different controller assumes responsibility, including a briefing on system status, recent events, and outstanding items.
OPS follows a structured handover protocol on every shift change — documented, timestamped, and retained in the compliance record.
195.446(h) — Backup SCADA
Backup SCADA & Communication Systems
Operators must test backup SCADA systems at least once each calendar year, test internal communication plans annually, and verify that backup systems can adequately support operations during an outage.
OPS maintains hot-standby backup SCADA, conducts annual tests with documented outcomes, and verifies communication plans every 15 months.
195.446(i) — Annual Review
Annual CRM Program Review
Operators must conduct an annual review of the CRM program to determine effectiveness, identify deficiencies, and document any changes made, signed by senior management.
OPS prepares the annual CRM review report, documents all findings, and coordinates management sign-off — every calendar year without exception.
