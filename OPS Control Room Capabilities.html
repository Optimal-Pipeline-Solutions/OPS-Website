Control Room Operations & Compliance

Control room operations and compliance services for midstream pipelines

1 Who Operates Your Pipeline

Running a pipeline control room is not just a staffing question. It is a compliance obligation. The Alarm Management Plan, the controller qualification program, the fatigue mitigation plan, the MOC process, the monthly alarm reviews — all of it sits with whoever is operating the control room. When something changes on the pipeline, the control room has to respond to it operationally and document it compliantly. When a PHMSA inspector arrives, the control room is where they start.

OPS was built around that reality. Our controllers are fully qualified, full-time pipeline professionals who have held Lead Controller and Alarm Management Plan ownership at regulated operators. The compliance programs — CRM, ISA-18.2 alarm rationalization, document control, MOC — are maintained by the same organization that operates the pipeline. When your system changes, one team handles it. There is no gap between the people running your pipeline and the people accountable for it on paper.

4 Purpose-Built Compliance Platform

OPS operates its own proprietary compliance and alarm management platform built specifically around the regulatory obligations a pipeline control room carries. Monthly alarm reviews, annual assessments, ISA-18.2 KPI tracking, point-to-point verification records, and revision-controlled compliance documentation are generated, tracked, and stored automatically.
