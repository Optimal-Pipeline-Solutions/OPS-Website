Service Overview

Automation Engineering & SCADA Services

Automation engineering services for midstream pipeline operations

1 Automation Engineering Practice

OPS maintains a dedicated in-house automation engineering practice with a combined 65-plus years of controls and automation experience across midstream pipeline operations — crude oil, natural gas, propane, and produced water. That experience spans greenfield builds, platform modernizations, and everything in between, with additional background in manufacturing and industrial systems.

Projects are delivered with full documentation: sequence of operations, as-built drawings, and operator materials completed before the system enters service. A PMP-certified project manager with 17 years of midstream delivery experience oversees every engagement from scope through commissioning.

2 SCADA Platforms

OPS engineers at a principal level on both Ignition and FactoryTalk — two of the most widely deployed platforms in midstream pipeline operations. The team also has working experience on Wonderware, iFIX, Geo SCADA, and other platforms commonly found in pipeline and midstream facilities. If your operation is running it, we have likely worked on it.
