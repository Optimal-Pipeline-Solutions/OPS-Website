1 Who We Are

Optimal Pipeline Solutions specializes in industrial automation, controls integration, SCADA, communications, and operational technology solutions for pipeline, energy, utility, and industrial clients. We help customers improve reliability, visibility, safety, and efficiency through turnkey automation projects. Just as often, that means integrating new capabilities into systems already in service — modernizing, expanding, and tying in new equipment without disrupting live operations. Both are backed by a team with more than 65 years of combined automation engineering experience.

4 Representative Midstream Projects

Spanning greenfield builds and integration of new capability into live, in-service systems.

Multi-Terminal SCADA & Control Center
Four storage terminals and a central control center, delivered simultaneously
ControlLogix PLCs with Ignition SCADA running on fault-tolerant server hardware, interfaced to Omni flow computers, tank gauging systems, and Siemens / ABB drives. The control center added an offsite warm-standby DR server and dual operator consoles driving eight monitors each.
Mainline Pump & Metering Tie-In
New mainline pumps and metering integrated into an existing crude tank farm
Provided PLC and Omni flow computer panel design, fabrication, programming, and field startup and commissioning — while updating the existing facility to support the new system tie-ins with no loss of operational continuity.
Metering & Booster Station Standardization
Six metering facilities and three booster stations on a common standard
Developed ControlLogix programming and InTouch HMI applications, standardized the operator's interfaces to VFDs, MCCs, Omni flow computers, and protective relays, and delivered panel checkout plus field commissioning and SCADA data-exchange verification across every site.
Cloud-Hosted Command Center
Azure-hosted Ignition SCADA command center with mobile field access
Designed and commissioned a cloud-hosted Ignition system supporting command-center operations through a single "yoked" project for minimal-click navigation, with secure remote and mobile access to facility data delivered through Ignition Perspective.
New Pipeline Section & Shipper Tie-In
New pipeline section connecting an existing tank farm to a new shipper connection
Led design, development, and commissioning of the new section, integrating additional Allen-Bradley controllers into the existing control system and establishing reusable HMI and PLC standards using high-performance graphics to streamline future work.
