Control Room Operations & Compliance

24/7/365 third-party control room operations and PHMSA compliance for midstream pipelines

1 Who We Are

Optimal Pipeline Solutions provides 24/7/365 third-party pipeline control room operations and PHMSA control room management compliance for crude oil, natural gas, and produced water operators. We help operators run safely and reliably, meet their regulatory obligations, and stay audit-ready — with the people monitoring the pipeline, the people maintaining the compliance program, and the people changing the system all under one roof. Our team brings decades of combined control room and pipeline compliance experience, including direct accountability as Control Room Manager at regulated operators and as the company representative in PHMSA inspections.

3 Platform & Systems Expertise

Pipeline SCADA Operations
Controllers experienced operating regulated crude oil, natural gas, and produced water systems on the major midstream SCADA platforms — enterprise pipeline SCADA, Ignition, and FactoryTalk-based control environments. We operate on your platform.
Leak Detection
CPM and non-CPM monitoring — line balance, real-time transient model (RTTM), and pressure/flow methods per API RP 1130 — on crude oil and hazardous liquid systems.
Alarm Management
ISA-18.2 alarm rationalization with monthly reviews, annual assessments, and KPI tracking; alarm journal analysis and nuisance / bad-actor reduction across multi-site systems.
Compliance & Records Platform
Proprietary OPS compliance and alarm-management platform — CRM documentation, monthly alarm reviews, point-to-point verification records, and revision-controlled documents with second-retrieval audit readiness. Purpose-built, not off-the-shelf.
Control Room Infrastructure
Redundant power, hardware, software, and network infrastructure built to the standards we hold regulated operators to; disaster-recovery / backup control center available as primary or standby.
