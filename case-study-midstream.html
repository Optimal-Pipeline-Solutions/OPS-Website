The Engagement

OPS serves as the dedicated control room for a major midstream pipeline and gathering operator running Ignition SCADA across dozens of remote sites handling crude oil, produced water, natural gas, and NGL products. This is not a contract with a start and end date — it is a continuous, evolving partnership where OPS engineering, compliance, and control room operations function as a single integrated team alongside the customer.

That integration is what makes the engagement work. Our engineers, compliance staff, and controllers collaborate daily — with each other and with the customer — to identify new ways to view operational data, streamline workflows, and close gaps before they become problems. When the customer needs something, they're not submitting a ticket to a vendor. They're talking to the people who already understand their operation, and those people are already working on it.

Our engineering team brings decades of controls experience that extends well beyond pipelines — into manufacturing, processing, and industrial environments where uptime isn't optional. That cross-industry depth means we approach problems differently than a team that has only ever worked in one sector. We've seen the patterns before, in other contexts, and we bring those solutions here.

Once a change is identified and the Management of Change process is complete, our team moves fast. Implementation happens in hours or days rather than weeks or months. The customer gets what they need, when they need it, built by people who understand both the system and the operation it supports.

Custom Tooling

Built for the operation.

Every tool in our platform was born from a real operational need on this engagement. What started as one-off solutions became a full compliance and operations suite.

Alarm Rationalization

Full ISA-18.2 rationalization workflow. Operators classify alarms and document cause, consequence, and corrective action — assisted by intelligent tooling that drafts documentation for human review. The compliance officer makes every judgment call and signs every record. Over 15,000 alarms rationalized and maintained across the entire system.

Background Data Engine

A background service running 24/7 that computes alarm metrics, imports phone logs, syncs scheduling data, indexes compliance documents, and generates monthly reports — all without operator intervention. The engine that keeps the compliance data current.

Controller Scheduling

Modified DuPont rotation with Hours-of-Service compliance tracking. Automated violation detection, rolling hour calculations, and reset monitoring — ensuring no controller exceeds PHMSA fatigue limits.

Document Control

Revision-controlled compliance document registry. Every P2P verification, alarm rationalization report, monthly review, and annual review is numbered, tracked, and audit-retrievable. The authoritative record system for every compliance deliverable.

Intelligent Tooling

A system this size generates millions of records — alarm events, operator actions, process values, shift logs, communications records. We integrate AI to help us analyze that volume and surface what matters: patterns in alarm performance, gaps in documentation, anomalies that a human reviewer should look at. The AI doesn't make decisions — it helps us make better ones, faster.

Audit Readiness

When PHMSA calls, we're ready. Every compliance document is retrievable by number, every alarm has a rationalization record, every controller shift has an HOS audit trail. The system was designed from the start to answer inspector questions in real time.

SCADA Engineering

60+ custom Ignition script modules written and maintained by OPS. Real-time line balance monitoring across crude, produced water, gas, and NGL streams. SCADA permissive control for delivery points. Communications status monitoring for 149 sites. Multi-monitor display management for controllers. Automated trend generation. This isn't off-the-shelf SCADA — it's custom engineering built directly into the system.

Multi-System Architecture

The platform supports multiple independent gathering systems under a single control center — each with its own site types, product streams, and operational patterns. Adding a new system doesn't require rebuilding anything. The architecture was designed from day one to scale horizontally as the operator's portfolio grows.

Evolution

How it grew.

This engagement didn't start with a grand plan. It started with monitoring. Every tool, every automation, every compliance capability was added because the operation needed it — and that's the point.

Foundation

24/7 Remote Monitoring

OPS takes over as the dedicated control room. Controllers monitor pipeline operations around the clock using Ignition Vision clients. The relationship starts here — reliable, responsive, always on.

Compliance

CRM Plan & Alarm Rationalization

PHMSA compliance requirements drive the need for a full CRM plan, alarm rationalization, and fatigue management. OPS builds the initial compliance framework and begins rationalizing the alarm population across all sites.

Automation

Custom Tooling & Data Pipelines

Manual compliance processes hit scale limits. OPS builds automated data pipelines — phone log imports, alarm metrics computation, shift report generation, document indexing — replacing hours of manual work with a background engine that runs continuously.

Intelligence

Intelligent Tooling

With millions of alarm events, process records, and operational data points accumulating across 149 sites, manual analysis doesn't scale. AI is integrated into our workflows to help us cut through that volume — identifying trends, flagging anomalies, and assembling documentation drafts from operational data. Our people review everything. The tooling makes sure they're looking at the right things.

Scale

Continuous Growth

New sites are added regularly. Each one gets integrated into the SCADA system, its alarms rationalized, its compliance documentation generated. The tooling handles the scale — what used to take weeks per site now takes days. The platform grows with the operator.
