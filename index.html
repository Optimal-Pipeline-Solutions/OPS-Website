Our Capabilities

What we deliver.

Pipeline operations demand more than isolated services—they require a partner who can monitor and control systems around the clock, respond instantly to operational events, stay ahead of compliance requirements, and engineer the infrastructure that connects it all.

24/7 Monitoring & Callout

Our controllers provide continuous, around-the-clock monitoring of pipeline, gathering, and terminal operations, ensuring your assets remain under constant supervision.

With dedicated staffing 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, we deliver uninterrupted coverage that enhances reliability, minimizes downtime, and reduces operational risk.

Whether your operation requires full remote control of critical assets or simply alarm monitoring and field callout services, we offer scalable solutions tailored to your operational requirements. From single facilities to complex multi-asset systems, our services are designed to provide the level of support, oversight, and responsiveness that best fits your organization's needs.

Control Room Operations

Our controllers provide professional oversight backed by real-world operational experience. With backgrounds spanning pipeline control, field operations, military service, and law enforcement, our team brings a disciplined approach to monitoring, communication, and incident response.

This depth of experience translates into greater situational awareness, sound decision-making, and consistent execution in both routine and abnormal operating conditions. More than simply monitoring alarms, our controllers understand the operational impact of events and the importance of timely, accurate response to support the safe and reliable operation of your assets.

CRM Compliance Management

The full PHMSA compliance stack — ISA-18.2 alarm rationalization, monthly alarm performance reviews, annual alarm reviews, safety-related alarm reviews, controller fatigue management, point-to-point verification, and revision-controlled document management. Our compliance team has managed these programs since the birth of the CRM rule, and purpose-built tooling generates and tracks every deliverable automatically.

Compliance Consulting

Third-party compliance consulting for operators who need expertise without a long-term contract. CRM plan development and review, mock audits, gap analysis, documentation assessment, and direct audit representation. Our team has successfully navigated control room audits from regulatory agencies including PHMSA — we know exactly what they look for and how to be ready for it.

SCADA & Automation Engineering

Our automation and control system services are available as part of a complete OPS Control Center solution or as a standalone engagement. From greenfield projects to system upgrades and modernization efforts, we design, integrate, and commission solutions tailored to each operation's needs.

Our expertise spans SCADA, PLCs, control panels, communications, field commissioning, and system integration across a wide range of industrial applications. Whether supporting a control center, pumping station, custody transfer facility, storage terminal, or other critical infrastructure, we deliver practical solutions backed by proven engineering and field experience.

Purpose-Built Compliance & Operations Software

When the off-the-shelf tools failed to fit our needs, we built our own. Automation

Featured projects.

OPS delivers reliable, scalable automation solutions built to meet the demands of complex operations — from initial PLC design to comprehensive system integration.

Terminal Automation

Rail-to-Storage and Automated Truck Rack System

Managed the offloading of 80 rail cars into storage through three dedicated product meter skids. Automated multi-flowrate proving, direct integration with TMS6 for batch and transaction data, and an interface with Toptech Multiloads for fully unattended driver operations at an eight-bay truck rack.

SCADA Modernization

Enterprise SCADA for Manufacturing

Enterprise-wide SCADA system using Ignition and Kepware OPC, replacing legacy systems. Centralized historical data for materials, batching, and forecasting with custom diagnostic tools that significantly reduced equipment downtime. SQL-driven configuration backend enabled rapid operational adjustments.

Multi-Site Deployment

Multi-Terminal Control and SCADA

Control systems for four terminals and a central control center using ControlLogix PLCs and Ignition SCADA on Stratus ftServers. Integrated with Omni flow computers, Raptor tank gauging, and Siemens/ABB VFDs. Standardized operational control with fault-tolerant server infrastructure.

HMI Modernization

High-Performance HMI Consolidation

Overhauled existing HMI systems using FactoryTalk View SE Network Distributed application built to high-performance standards. Consolidated three standalone HMIs into a single Stratus server utilizing virtual machines and a unified HMI platform.

Purpose-Built Software

OPS Control Room Management Platform

A proprietary operations management platform developed specifically to support pipeline control room compliance under 49 CFR 195.446, 49 CFR 192.631, and ISA-18.2. Modules span alarm rationalization, automated monthly and annual alarm performance reviews, safety-related alarm reviews, controller scheduling with HOS compliance tracking, dynamic point-to-point verification with auto-generated controlled documents, and large-scale operational metrics collection and analysis. An integrated document control system maintains full revision history and audit-ready retrieval for every compliance deliverable. A purpose-built pipeline simulation environment supports development and integration testing.
