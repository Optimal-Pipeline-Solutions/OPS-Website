1 We Know Your Environment

Plains moves crude oil and NGL across roughly 18,000 miles of active pipeline, monitored and controlled from your enterprise SCADA and historian environment. Optimal Pipeline Solutions is built by people who have operated, automated, and audited exactly this kind of system. We do not compete with your host SCADA — we deliver the field automation, custody measurement, and compliance support that report up into it, with the documentation and discipline a regulated operator requires.

Where We Fit
Your control room runs on enterprise pipeline SCADA, with a historian layer for operational data and computational leak detection for line integrity. Our work lives at the field and station layer — the pump-station PLCs, custody metering, LACT units, and tank-farm controls that generate the data your operators and analysts depend on. Clean field automation and trustworthy measurement make everything upstream of the controller better.

New mainline pumps and metering integrated into an existing crude tank farm. PLC and Omni flow computer panel design, fabrication, programming, and field startup — while keeping the existing facility in service through the tie-ins.
Metering & Booster Station Standardization
Six metering facilities and three booster stations brought onto a common control standard — ControlLogix programming, standardized operator interfaces to VFDs, MCCs, and Omni flow computers, with panel checkout and field commissioning across every site.
Multi-Terminal SCADA & Control Center
Four storage terminals and a central control center delivered together — ControlLogix with SCADA on fault-tolerant servers, interfaced to Omni flow computers, tank gauging, and drives, with offsite DR and dual operator consoles.
New Pipeline Section & Shipper Tie-In
Design, development, and commissioning of a new pipeline section connecting an existing tank farm to a new shipper connection, with reusable HMI and PLC standards established to streamline follow-on work.
Why It Matters for a Regulated Operator
Every engagement closes with a complete handoff — as-built drawings, tag lists, and operator documentation current on the day your team takes the controls. Sequences of operation are written before commissioning; systems are tested before the crew is on site. For a PHMSA-regulated operator, that documentation discipline is not a nicety — it is what stands up in an audit.
