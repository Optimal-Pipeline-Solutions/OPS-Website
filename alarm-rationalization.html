How It Starts
Alarm configurations are exported directly from your SCADA system (Ignition or equivalent) and imported into OPS Alarm Rationalization. The system compares each import against the existing inventory — only new or changed alarms surface for review. Unchanged alarms carry forward automatically, keeping your workload focused and your history intact.
